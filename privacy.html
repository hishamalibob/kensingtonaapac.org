Privacy and accessibility

How this site treats your information, and how we make it work for everyone

Kensington AAPAC is a volunteer group of parents and caregivers. This page explains what kensingtonaapac.org collects, how we use what families share with us, and what to do if something on the site doesn't work for you.

What this website collects

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Our fonts and photos are served from this site, so reading it doesn't send your information to other companies.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. AAPAC doesn't see those records.

Our forms

Our forms are Google Forms. When you open one, you are on Google's site, and Google's privacy policy applies. Answer only what you are comfortable sharing.

Responses go to AAPAC's email account. We use them only to plan AAPAC's work and to reach you if you asked us to. We never sell or share them, we never publish names, and we never name children.

Email and our calendar

Messages to hello@kensingtonaapac.org are forwarded to AAPAC's email account. If you subscribe to our calendar, your calendar app checks this site for new dates. We can't see who subscribes.

Photos

We share photos from AAPAC events to celebrate our community, and we credit the photographer. If you would like a photo of your child removed, write to us and we will take it down the same day.

Accessibility

We want every family to be able to use this site. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We check the site with automated accessibility tests and with a keyboard alone, on phones and computers, in light and dark mode.

If something doesn't work for you, or you would like information in another format or language, tell us and we will help.

Write to hello@kensingtonaapac.org.
